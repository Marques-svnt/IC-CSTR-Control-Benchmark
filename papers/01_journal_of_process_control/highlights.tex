\item Sign-blind clamping, a frequent error, latches reverse-acting loops at full cooling
\item A slew-rate trigger restores the loop; a slew-limit map shows when it matters

\item Exact-loop Ms bound gives 11\% ITAE in sample and none out of sample
\item Valve noise gain follows |Kp|(1+N), not the exact-loop sensitivity peak
